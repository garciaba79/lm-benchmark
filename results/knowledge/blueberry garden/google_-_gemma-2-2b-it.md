Here's some advice on improving your blueberry garden, pest/disease control, watering, and fertilizer:

**Improving Your Blueberry Garden:**

* **Soil Testing:**  Regularly test your soil pH (ideally every year) to ensure it remains within the optimal range of 4.5-5.5 for blueberries. Amend with lime if needed to raise pH levels.
* **Mulching & Weed Control:** Consider a thicker layer of mulch (3 inches or more) than pine bark, like wood chips or shredded leaves, which retain moisture better and suppress weeds effectively.
* **Sunlight:**  Blueberries prefer full sun, ideally 6-8 hours per day. If you have limited sunlight, consider planting in an area that receives the most sun possible.

**Pest & Disease Control:**

* **Aphids:** Neem oil is effective against aphids. Other natural methods include insecticidal soap or a strong spray of water from your hose to dislodge them.
* **Other Pests:** Be aware of potential pests like spider mites, slugs, and Japanese beetles.  Early detection and appropriate action are crucial for preventing significant damage. 
    * Monitor plants regularly and take measures when needed. 
* **Common Diseases:** Watch out for powdery mildew (white powdery spots on leaves), blueberry leaf spot (dark brown lesions) and root rot. Proper drainage is essential to prevent these diseases.

**Watering Your Blueberries:**

* **Deep Watering:**  Blueberries prefer deep, infrequent watering that encourages roots to grow deeper. 
    * Water deeply in the morning to allow for good water absorption before sun exposure can evaporate moisture quickly.
* **Mulch Benefits:** Mulching helps retain soil moisture and reduces evaporation. This makes your blueberry plants more resilient to droughts.

**Fertilizer Recommendations:**

* **Ammonium sulfate (AS):**  Excellent choice for blueberries, especially in spring. It provides readily available nitrogen for strong early growth. Follow the instructions on the package for application rates. 
    * Research other options like granular or slow-release fertilizers with a balanced NPK formula and apply according to label recommendations.

 **Additional Tips:**
 * Maintain good air circulation around your blueberry plants by pruning away any dense foliage.
