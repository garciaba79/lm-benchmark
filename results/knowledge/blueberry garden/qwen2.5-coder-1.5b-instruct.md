2. **Scale Insects**: Scale insects can also be a problem, especially on older plants. Treat with a mixture of 70% water and dish soap, or use an organic pesticide like horticultural oil.

### Disease Management

1. **Blueberry Leaf Blight (BBL)**: BBL is caused by the fungus Phytophthora rubra. It can be managed with a combination of cultural practices, such as proper watering and spacing, and fungicides.
2. **Black Spot**: Black spot is another common disease that causes leaves to turn black and drop off. Use a fungicide like Mancozeb or chlorothalonil to control it.
