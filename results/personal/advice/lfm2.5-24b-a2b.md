> #### 🤖 Assistant

1. **Build a 12–18 month financial runway that includes self-only health insurance premiums.** At $4k/month you need enough cash to cover personal expenses *and* business costs for almost a year, plus the higher, solo rates of ACA or short-term medical coverage if you drop your employer plan. Without this buffer, inconsistent income becomes stressful rather than exciting.

If the pipeline dries up after you leave, you'll have no choice but to scramble anyway. Also, decide now how many hours per week you'll work and stick to it so burnout doesn't sneak in during the transition.

These two steps directly address your three concerns: insurance comes from the first; income inconsistency is reduced by the second; and a predictable revenue stream + set boundaries protect against burnout when you make the leap.
