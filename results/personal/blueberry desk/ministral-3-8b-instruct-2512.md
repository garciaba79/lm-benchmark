### **1. Milling & Preparing Blueberry Wood**
#### **Key Challenges & Solutions:**
- **Thick Trunks (Up to 6")**:
  - Use a **ripping chain** designed for hardwoods on your bandsaw to reduce tear-out.
  - For small, irregular pieces:
    - **Flatten one face first**: Clamp the piece to a stable surface and use a hand plane or jointer to create a flat reference. This helps with subsequent cuts.
    - **Custom jig for bandsaw ripping**:
      - Secure the wood in a vise or rig to prevent wobble during cuts. For curved/irregular pieces, make incremental passes (e.g., 1–2" at a time) and flip the piece to maintain straightness.
      - *Alternative*: Use a **table saw** with a push block for straighter cuts if bandsaw control is difficult.

- **Drying & Cracking**:
  - Air-dry slowly in a controlled environment (e.g., 50–70°F, ~50% humidity) to prevent checking/cracking. Thicker pieces may need kiln drying or gradual stacking with spacers.
  - Once dry, **pre-drill** for screws/nails if needed to avoid splitting.

- **Waste Management**:
  - Glue-ups will produce scraps—save small pieces for:
    - **Edge banding** (if using edge-grain top).
    - **Inlays or decorative accents** (e.g., contrasting grain patterns).

### **4. Step-by-Step Milling Plan**
1. **Flatten All Pieces**:
   - Use a hand plane or jointer on one face of every slab (even small pieces) to create a reference for milling.
2. **Rip to Width**:
   - For thick slabs (>3"), rip into **2–3" widths** first, then flatten each piece again if needed.
   - For thin pieces (1–2"), clamp to a stable surface and use the bandsaw with slow feed.
3. **Thickness Planing**:
   - Set your planer to **slightly under desired thickness** (e.g., 0.75" for aprons) and pass pieces through multiple times to avoid tear-out.
4. **Drying Check**:
   - Test moisture content with a meter (<12% ideal). If too high, air-dry further before final cuts.
5. **Glue-Ups**:
   - For tops/aprons:
     - Use **waterproof glue** (e.g., Titebond III) and clamps.
     - Add **scrap wood spacers** between layers to maintain thickness.
     - Sand all surfaces **before gluing** for smooth joints.
