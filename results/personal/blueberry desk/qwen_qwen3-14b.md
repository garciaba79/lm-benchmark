### **3. Grain Orientation for the Desk Top**  
- **Face-Grain**:  
  - Pros: Showcases the fine grain patterns and figure of blueberry wood.  
  - Cons: Slightly more prone to warping if not dried perfectly; requires careful planning to avoid cupping.  

- **Edge-Grain**:  
  - Pros: Extremely stable (less likely to warp or crack), ideal for a minimalist design. The linear grain pattern adds subtle texture.  
  - Cons: Less dramatic visual appeal compared to face-grain.  

- **End-Grain**:  
  - Pros: Durable and resistant to dents; unique, "puzzle-like" appearance.  
  - Cons: Difficult to glue up (requires precise alignment) and may not be ideal for a smooth surface.  

**Recommendation**: Opt for **edge-grain** if stability is critical, or **face-grain** if you prioritize showcasing the wood’s figure. If using face-grain, ensure the wood is fully dried and apply a thin layer of finish (e.g., shellac) to seal it before assembly.
