Options trading is like buying the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell an underlying asset (like stocks) at a predetermined price within a specific time frame. It’s a way to hedge risks or gain extra exposure without directly owning the asset itself. For example, you might use call options to protect against falling stock prices or put options to lock in future price drops. The key is understanding the different types of options (call vs. put) and how they work with market conditions.
