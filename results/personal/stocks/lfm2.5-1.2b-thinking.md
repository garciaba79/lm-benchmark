> #### 💬 User

Explain options trading in simple terms if I'm familiar with buying and selling stocks.

> #### 🤖 Assistant

Options trading lets you buy the *right* to buy/sell an asset at a set price later (called a "strike price"). For example, if you own a stock and think it’ll rise:  
- A **call option** lets you *buy* the stock at a lower future price.  
- A **put option** gives you the right to *sell* it at that price.  

You control the outcome without owning the asset outright, adding flexibility but also complexity and risk.
