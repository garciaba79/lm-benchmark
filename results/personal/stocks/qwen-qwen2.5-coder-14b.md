> #### 💬 User

Explain options trading in simple terms if I'm familiar with buying and selling stocks.

> #### 🤖 Assistant

Options trading is like renting an option to buy or sell a stock at a specific price within a certain time frame. Here are the key points:

1. Types of options:
   - Call options: Right to buy the stock at a set price (strike price)
   - Put options: Right to sell the stock at a set price

2. How it works:
   - You buy an option for a fee called a premium
   - You don't have to exercise the option; you can let it expire worthless
   - If you exercise, you follow through with the buy/sell at the strike price

3. Advantages:
   - Lower cost than buying/selling shares outright
   - Can profit from stock movement without owning it
   - Limits potential losses

4. Risks:
   - Premium paid can be lost if option expires unused
   - Unlimited potential loss on puts (if stock rises)
   - Limited profit potential compared to stocks

5. Example:
   - Buy a call option with strike price $100 for $10 premium
   - If stock rises above $100, you can exercise and sell at higher price than $100 + $10 premium

Remember, options are complex financial instruments. Always do your research before trading.
