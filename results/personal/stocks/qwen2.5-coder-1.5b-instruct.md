> #### 🤖 Assistant

Options trading is a financial market activity where you can buy or sell the right to purchase or sell an asset at a predetermined price on a specified date. It's different from stock trading because it involves buying and selling contracts, not actual ownership of the underlying asset.
Here are some key points about options trading:
1. Types of options: There are two main types of options: call options and put options. A call option gives you the right to buy an asset at a predetermined price, while a put option gives you the right to sell an asset at a predetermined price.
2. Premium: The cost of buying or selling an option is called the premium. It's the amount you pay for the right to purchase or sell the underlying asset at the specified price.
3. Exercise: You can exercise your options at any time before the expiration date, but it's important to note that exercising options can result in significant losses if the underlying asset doesn't move as expected.
4. Risk management: Options trading involves a high degree of risk, so it's important to carefully consider your investment goals and risk tolerance before entering the market.
5. Professional advice: It's always a good idea to consult with a financial advisor or broker before making any options trades.
Overall, options trading can be a powerful tool for investors who want to speculate on the future price of an asset or hedge against potential losses. However, it requires careful analysis and understanding of the market to make informed decisions.
