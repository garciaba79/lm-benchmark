### **Day 1: Gulf Coast Vibes & Fresh Seafood**

**Morning: Hit the Beach (Free)**
Start your day at **St. Joseph Peninsula State Park**. It's one of the few state parks on the Florida Panhandle with a beautiful white-sand beach and dunes. You can just walk along the shoreline, look for shells, or rent a cheap bike from the local shops if you want to explore further.
*   *Cost:* $0 (parking is usually around $5–$7).

**Lunch: The Classic Shrimp & Grits Experience**
Head over to **The Crab Shack**. It's right on the water and has that classic, no-frills Gulf Coast feel. Order their famous shrimp and grits or a fish sandwich. It's messy, delicious, and incredibly affordable.
*   *Cost:* ~$18–$20 for a hearty meal + drink.

**Afternoon: Relax & People Watch**
Instead of rushing to another attraction, just find a shady spot at the **St. Joseph Peninsula State Park picnic area** or wander around the small downtown area near the pier. Grab an iced coffee from **Cafe St. Joe** (a local favorite) and people-watch. The energy here is so chill it'll reset your brain.
*   *Cost:* ~$5 for a drink/snack.

### **Day 2: History, Nature & Sunset Dinner**

**Morning: Explore the History**
Drive over to **Wakulla Springs State Park**. Even if you don't go swimming (which is great too!), just walking along the boardwalks near the spring headwaters is magical. The water is crystal clear and naturally filtered by limestone. It feels like stepping into a fairy tale.
*   *Cost:* ~$10 for parking/entry.

**Lunch: Local Favorites**
Stop at **The Old Florida Restaurant & Bar**. They have great Southern comfort food, and the atmosphere is super friendly. Try their chicken fried steak or a classic burger. It's not fancy, but it hits the spot perfectly after a morning hike.
*   *Cost:* ~$15–$18 for lunch + drink.

**Afternoon: Nature Walk & Bird Watching**
Head to **Wakulla River State Park**. This is a hidden gem! You can take a free guided kayak tour (check their website, usually around $20 per person) OR just rent a bike and explore the trails yourself. If you skip the tour, just walk the boardwalks—it's stunningly beautiful with tons of wildlife.
*   *Cost:* Either ~$20 for a kayak tour or ~$15 to rent bikes. Let's go with the kayak tour for that unique experience.

**Evening: Sunset & Simple Farewell Dinner**
End your trip at **The Crab Shack** again (or try **Burger Joint** if you're craving something different). Grab a table by the water, watch the sun dip below the horizon over the Gulf, and enjoy some simple fried seafood or a burger. There's nothing quite like watching the Florida sunset from a beachside shack.
*   *Cost:* ~$18–$20 for dinner + drink.
