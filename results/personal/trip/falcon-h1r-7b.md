## 🎁 Pro‑Tips from Your Friend Who Lives Here

| Tip | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| **Buy groceries at Walmart/Publix before heading out** – you can stock up on fresh shrimp, avocado, fruit, chips and sandwich fixings for under $10. Then bring a cooler to the beach or park for free picnics. | Saves money vs. eating out every meal. |
| **Reserve kayak rentals early in the morning** – they fill up fast on weekends! A quick call at 8 am secures your slot and avoids surprise price hikes later. | Guarantees you can paddle without missing out. |
| **Use the Pensacola Bay Visitor Center discount booklet** (available at the harbor kiosk). It offers 10‑15% off on restaurant meals that accept the code, plus free guided tours of historic sites. | Little savings add up quickly. |
| **Pack a reusable water bottle and bring your own sunscreen/hat**. Beach fees are zero but you’ll stay comfortable and avoid buying extras at the beach shack. | Keeps costs low and reduces plastic waste. |
| **Check tide tables for West Ship Island ferry** (online or via the park website). Going on a high tide means less muddy footing and easier docking—plus you’ll know when to schedule your activity best. | Prevents wasted travel time if the ferry isn’t running due to low water. |
| **If you want fresh fish without paying restaurant prices, ask at the local fish market on Main St.** They often sell whole snapper or grouper for under $12/lb and will clean it for you (or give instructions). Then grill it back at your Airbnb! | Great way to eat like locals while staying cheap. |
| **Don’t forget the free parking lots in downtown Crestview.** They’re close to most restaurants, shops, and beaches, so you avoid any pricey garage fees. | Saves a few bucks on incidentals. |
