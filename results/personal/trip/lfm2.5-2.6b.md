> #### 🤖 Assistant

Oh man, Crestview is exactly the kind of place that feels like a real escape from the hustle. I love the vibe there: salty air, wide Gulf beaches, and small-town charm where you can actually hear the waves. And $300 for a whole weekend (gas not counting) gives us plenty of room to eat well *and* do something active without breaking a sweat.

Here’s my game plan, laid out like we’re grabbing coffee before hitting the road:

**Day 1: Sun, Seafood, and Saltwater Sunsets**

- **Morning (Breakfast):** Start at **The Coffee House & Café** on Main Street. They do solid pastries and good drip coffee for around **$10**. It’s cozy and locals love it.
- **Mid-morning:** Walk over to **Blue Water Bridge Park**. You get the pier, some great Gulf views, and if you’re lucky, dolphins or a pelican flying by. Completely free. Grab a quick snack from the nearby stand (maybe a frozen smoothie for **$7**) while you soak in the scenery.
- **Lunch:** Head to **Dockside Grille** on the waterfront. They’ve got fish tacos and clam chowder that won’t break the bank; a combo lunch is about **$15‑20**. The patio overlooks the pier, which is perfect for people-watching.
- **Afternoon:** After eating, wander over to **Sandy Beach** (just past the bridge) for some barefoot time and sun. If you want a little more adventure, you could rent a paddleboard from one of the local shops (around $30‑40 per hour), but it’s optional.
- **Dinner:** For your second meal, try **Margaritavine by the Bay** on the waterfront. It’s a bit pricier than the others ($25‑$35 for a main and drink), but that’s where you get to sit on deck with the sunset. I’d say $20 per person if you’re sharing an appetizer and main.
- **Evening:** Stroll along the boardwalk one more time, maybe pick up some local honey or a small souvenir at a stand (under **$10**).

**Day 2: Kayaking and Chill**

- **Breakfast:** Hit up **Crusty’s Restaurant & Bar** for a hearty breakfast—think eggs, bacon, and home-made biscuits. Around **$12**.
- **Morning Activity:** **Kayak or paddleboard rental** from **Crestview Kayak Co.** (right off the waterfront). A two-hour session is roughly **$35**. You’ll glide through calm bay waters and see dolphins if they’re in range. If you prefer to save, just relax on the beach until lunch.
- **Lunch:** Grab something casual at **Piney Point Preserve** food trucks (they often have tacos or salads for **$10‑12**). Or, if you want a sit-down feel, **The Olde Town Pub** is actually in Pensacola but a 5-minute drive from Crestview and does decent seafood for under $20.
- **Afternoon:** After lunch, take it easy. Maybe revisit Blue Water Bridge Park or just lounge on the sand. If you want a little extra adventure, there’s a nature trail loop near the park that’s free and great for spotting birds.
- **Dinner (final night):** One last bite at **Sea Salt Bar & Grill** if you haven’t tried it yet; they have a solid brunch menu that works nicely late as well. Or stick with Dockside again for consistency.

That leaves you about **$160 in the bank**. You could splurge on a nicer dinner, pick up a souvenir from the Crestview market, or just save it for your next trip. The plan is totally flexible though—swap the kayak if you want to spend more time sunbathing instead.

**Pro tip:** Arrive early Saturday morning so you can beat the crowds at Blue Water Bridge and dock at Dockside Grille. And don’t forget sunscreen; Gulf Coast sun is intense even in shoulder season.
