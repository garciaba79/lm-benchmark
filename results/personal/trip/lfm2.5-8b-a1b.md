## DAY 1 – “Arrival + Outdoor Adventure”

### Morning  
- **Check‑in** (drop your bags at the motel/Airbnb). Many places in Crestview charge **$70–$80/night**; you can shave $10 by booking an Airbnb a few blocks from the park.  

### Mid‑Morning Snack  
- Grab a coffee and a **fresh fruit cup** or a quick pastry at *The Original Pancake House* (≈ **$12** for two). It’ll fuel the hike ahead.

### 9:30 am – Hike the **Choctawhatchee State Park Loop** (3 mi, mostly flat)  
- **Park entry:** $5 per vehicle. The trail winds through pine forests and offers a quiet creek crossing—great for photos if you’re feeling Instagram‑ready.  

### Lunch  
- Head down to *Bobby’s Seafood & Bar* on the waterfront. Order two **pan‑fried fish tacos** or a shrimp po’ boy combo (≈ **$22**) plus drinks. It’s casual, tasty, and right by the park (free parking).

### Early Afternoon – Cool Down  
- Take a short drive to *Mary Ann Park* for a quick dip in the Gulf‑tide pools or a walk along the shoreline. Bring a towel you already have—no extra cost.

### Late Lunch / Light Snack  
- If you’re still hungry, stop at *The Beach House Café* for a **fish‑and‑chips bowl** (≈ **$15**) and some local craft soda.

### Evening – Chill & Dinner  

| Time | Spot | What to Order | Approx. Cost |
|------|------|---------------|--------------|
| 6 pm | *Rustic Grille & Bar* (downtown) | **Grilled chicken or veggie flatbread** + salad + share a **local craft beer** (≈ **$30**) | $30 |

- After dinner, take an easy stroll through downtown Crestview’s boutique shops and maybe swing by the *Crestview Visitor Center* for free maps. If you’re up for it, catch a sunset from the park’s picnic tables—no admission fee.

## DAY 2 – “Water, Wind & West‑Coast Vibes”

### Sunrise Walk / Beach Time  
- **Sunrise at Henderson Beach State Park** (just 15 min north). No entry fee; just bring a blanket and enjoy the Gulf sunrise. Parking is free but fills up early—arrive by **5:30 am**.

### Breakfast on‑the‑go  
- Pick up an **overnight oatmeal or breakfast sandwich** from *Mama’s Kitchen* (≈ **$10**) to go.

### Mid‑Morning Kayak / Paddleboard Session  
- Rent a single kayak (or paddleboard) at the nearby marina for **1 hour**. Local outfitters charge about **$30 per hour**, so $30 total. It’s a gentle glide through the Choctawhatchee River—perfect for spotting herons and maybe some dolphins in the distance.

### Lunch  
- Head back to downtown and eat at *The Gulf Coast Café* (think shrimp‑avocado salad or chicken Caesar) – **$20** for two. Their “share plates” are a budget‑friendly way to split food.

### Afternoon Relaxation  
- **Option A: Bike the 5‑mile paved path from Henderson Beach up through town to the historic downtown.** Rent bikes at *Crestview Cycle & Travel* for **$15** (hourly). You’ll see quirky shops, art galleries, and maybe a quick coffee stop.  

- **Option B: Free time** – nap on the beach, read, or simply relax on your lodging’s patio with a cold drink.

### Late Afternoon Snack / Ice Cream  
- Grab an **ice cream sundae** from *J & J Frozen* (≈ **$8**)—a sweet finish before you head home.  

### Departure  
- Pack up, lock the car, and on your way out, swing by the *Okaloosa County Historical Museum* (admission $5) if you have a spare few bucks; it’s a nice little cultural bite.

## QUICK TIPS & PERSONAL TOUCHES

1. **Book early** – Motels fill up fast in peak spring/fall, but even off‑season there are often deals under $80/night.  
2. **Bring your own water bottle and snacks** (granola bars, fruit) to shave a few more dollars on food.  
3. **Carry cash for small vendors** – some little cafés prefer it over cards.  
4. **Check the weather** before you go; Crestview can be breezy in the mornings—layer up!  
5. **Don’t forget sunscreen, hat, and insect repellent** (the Gulf area loves its mosquitoes).
