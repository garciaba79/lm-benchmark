### **Day 1: Adventure & Local Flavor**
**Morning:**  
- **Breakfast at The Breakfast Club** (Crestview’s local favorite, super cozy and affordable). Try their “Southern Comfort” plate ($12) with biscuits, eggs, and sausage.  
- **Hike at Crestview Regional Park** (free!): Explore the trails, maybe spot some wildlife, and snap pics of the lake. Bring water and snacks from home to save money.  

**Afternoon:**  
- **Lunch at The Blue Plate Diner**: Their “Crestview Burger” ($10) is legendary, or go for a sandwich if you’re not in the mood for beef.  
- **Relax at Lake Crestview Park** (free): Rent a paddleboat ($15/hour for 2 hours—split with a friend!). If you prefer land, just bring a blanket and enjoy the sunset by the water.  

**Evening:**  
- **Dinner at The Local**: A farm-to-table spot with creative dishes. Try their “Crestview Plate” ($18) or a fresh salad if you’re lighter.  
- **Relax at a Local Brewery** (optional): If you’re up for it, check out **Crestview Brewing Company** (no cover charge, $5-8 for a craft beer).  

**Costs so far:**  
- Breakfast: $12  
- Lunch: $10  
- Paddleboat: $15  
- Dinner: $18  
- Drinks/snacks: $20  
**Total: ~$75** (leaves you $225 for extras!)
