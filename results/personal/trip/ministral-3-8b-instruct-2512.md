### **📅 Day 1: Adventure & Coastal Vibes**
**Theme:** *Kayak + Beach + Local Eats*

#### **Morning: Paddle Through Paradise**
- **Activity:** Rent kayaks at **Crestview Marina** (~$30–$50 for 2 hours).
  - *Why?* The **Apalachicola River** is right there, and you can paddle to **Little St. George Island** (just a short trip away) for a quick beach stop.
  - *Pro tip:* Bring water/snacks—there’s no food on the island, but the views are *chef’s kiss*.
- **Cost:** ~$40 total (2 kayaks + life jackets).

#### **Lunch: Fresh Seafood at The Docks**
- **Where:** [The Docks Restaurant](https://www.thedockscrestview.com/) (5 min drive from marina).
  - *Must-order:* Their **shrimp po’boys** or **blackened redfish**—fresh Gulf catch, no frills.
  - *Budget hack:* Split a seafood platter (~$20–$30 for two).
- **Cost:** ~$25.

#### **Afternoon: Relax at St. Joe’s Beach**
- **Activity:** Drive to **St. Joseph Peninsula State Park** (15 mins from Crestview).
  - *Why?* Free beach access, soft sand, and a short **boardwalk trail** through dunes (~$6 parking fee).
  - *Bonus:* Bring a blanket, a book, or just nap in the shade.
- **Cost:** ~$6.

#### **Dinner: BBQ & Backyard Vibes**
- **Where:** [The Pig & Whistle](https://www.pigandwhistle.com/) (10 mins from beach).
  - *Why?* Their **pulled pork sandwich** is legendary—smoky, tender, and served with mac ‘n’ cheese.
  - *Budget hack:* Go early to avoid crowds; share sides.
- **Cost:** ~$35 total.

#### **Evening: Sunset & Stargazing**
- **Activity:** Walk along the **Apalachicola Riverwalk** (free) or just sit on your hotel porch if you’re staying there.
  - *Bonus:* Crestview has *amazing* dark skies—grab a cheap blanket and spot constellations.

### **📅 Day 2: Forest & Farm Fun**
**Theme:** *Hike + Local Farms + Chill Coffee*

#### **Morning: Trail Magic at Big Bay State Park**
- **Activity:** Hike the **1.5-mile Pine Log Trail** (free entry, $6 parking).
  - *Why?* Shaded trails, wildlife spotting (deer! birds!), and a peaceful escape.
  - *Pack:* Water, sunscreen, and your camera.
- **Cost:** ~$6.

#### **Brunch: Sweet Tea & Pancakes**
- **Where:** [The Coffee Cup](https://www.thecoffeecupcrestview.com/) (downtown Crestview).
  - *Must-order:* Their **Southern pancake breakfast** or a giant biscuit.
  - *Budget hack:* Skip the fancy drinks—just coffee and tea.
- **Cost:** ~$20.

#### **Afternoon: Pick Your Own Peaches (Seasonal!)**
- **Where:** [Peach Valley Farm](https://www.peachvalleyfarm.com/) (~30 mins from Crestview).
  - *Why?* If you’re visiting in **June–August**, pick your own peaches for ~$2/lb.
  - *Non-seasonal option:* Visit a local **u-pick strawberry farm** (check [Florida’s seasonal guide](https://www.floridapikers.com/)).
- **Cost:** ~$15 (for fruit + snacks).

#### **Dinner: Casual & Creative**
- **Where:** [The Rustic Table](https://www.therustictablecrestview.com/) (downtown).
  - *Why?* Their **wood-fired pizzas** are a local favorite—try the "Crestview Special" with local cheeses.
  - *Budget hack:* Split a pizza + salad (~$30 for two).
- **Cost:** ~$30.

#### **Nightcap: Drinks by the River**
- **Where:** [The Wharf Bar & Grill](https://www.thewharfbar.com/) (waterfront views).
  - *Why?* Cheap local beer ($5–$6) and a fun vibe. Skip dinner here—just grab a drink.
- **Cost:** ~$10.
