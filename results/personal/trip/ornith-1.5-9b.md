## Day 1 — Beach & Bay Breeze

**Morning:** Check in and either nap it off or head straight to the beach. **Destin Beach** is about a 15-minute drive and has that gorgeous white sand and emerald water everyone raves about. If you want something quieter and free, **Niceville Beach** (another ~20 min) is less crowded and totally legit.

- *Cost: Free to enter*

**Lunch:** Grab something casual on the way back—there are plenty of spots along Water Street in downtown Crestview with seafood, sandwiches, and good coffee. I'd look for a spot like **The Waterfront Restaurant** if you want proper Gulf seafood (shrimp, crab, fish), or just grab a lighter bite to save money.

- *Cost: ~$15–25/person*

**Afternoon:** Rent a kayak or two and paddle the **Choctawhatchee River**, or just soak up the beach until sunset. Kayaking's usually around $40–60 for a half day depending on where you rent—worth it if you like being active, but honestly a lazy beach afternoon is equally valid here.

- *Cost: ~$50 max*

**Dinner:** Back downtown and make this your best meal. Water Street has some great options—seafood, steaks, or just good comfort food. Enjoy the walkable historic district at night; it's cozy and fun to stroll around after dinner.

- *Cost: ~$25–40/person*

## Day 2 — River & Last-Minute Treats

**Morning:** Do whatever your body wants—another beach, a sunrise paddle, or just slow coffee on Water Street. If you're feeling adventurous, the river's got some nice spots for fishing too if you've got gear (or rent tackle).

- *Cost: Free–$50*

**Lunch:** Casual and easy—grab something from downtown to keep it light.

- *Cost: ~$12–20/person*

**Afternoon:** Wander Water Street's shops, galleries, and little boutiques for some last-minute souvenirs or treats. Grab a dessert or coffee as your farewell.

- *Cost: ~$20–40 total*
