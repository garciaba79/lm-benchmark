### **Day 1 – Friday**

**Morning & Early Afternoon: Explore Nature at Lighthouse Point Park**
📍 *Where:* Just outside of Crestview — it's actually in Fort Walton Beach, which is a short drive away.  
⏰ Time: 9 AM to noon or so  
💰 Cost: Free! (Just bring your own stuff)  

- **Why go:** This place has a lighthouse, boardwalks through the marshes, a nature center, and even some kayak rentals if you're feeling adventurous.
- **Pro tip:** Bring a picnic blanket, and hit up the park for a morning walk or bike ride. The views of Choctawhatchee Bay are stunning.

---

**Lunch: The Fishery (Fort Walton Beach)**
📍 *Where:* 513 East Main Street  
💰 Cost: ~$20–$30 total (split between two people, depending on what you order)

- **Why eat here:** It's a local favorite with killer fish tacos, crab cakes, and a great view of the harbor. They even have a bar for drinks if you're in the mood.
- **Try this combo:** Grilled shrimp skewers + sweet potato fries + a cold drink.

---

**Afternoon: Relax at Blue Wave Beach**
📍 *Where:* Just south of Lighthouse Point (again, Fort Walton Beach)  
⏰ Time: 1 PM to 4 PM  

- **Why go:** This is one of the most underrated beaches in the area. It's calm, family-friendly, and perfect for a long walk or reading in the sand.
- **Pro tip:** Pack a reusable water bottle — there are no vendors here, but it’s a great spot for peace and quiet.

---

**Dinner: Moe's Original BBQ (Crestview)**
📍 *Where:* 1038 N US Highway 98  
💰 Cost: ~$25–$30 total (split between two)

- **Why eat here:** This spot is a local gem with smoky, flavorful BBQ ribs and brisket. They have a full menu of sides, salads, and even some vegan options.
- **Try this combo:** Half-rack ribs + mac & cheese + a side salad.

---

**Evening: Chill at the Crestview Community Center or Downtown**
📍 *Where:* downtown Crestview (a few blocks from the main drag)  
⏰ Time: 6 PM onwards  

- **Why do it:** Take a stroll around downtown, grab some live music if there’s something on, or just sit outside with a drink and people-watch.
- **Pro tip:** If you’re feeling fancy but still want to stay under budget, check out the **Crestview Farmer's Market** (if it's open — usually on weekends) for cheap eats like gourmet hot dogs or fresh fruit.

---

### **Day 2 – Saturday**

**Morning: Kayaking or Paddleboarding at Choctawhatchee Bay**
📍 *Where:* Lighthouse Point Park / Choose Your Own Adventure (kayaks & paddleboards available there too)
⏰ Time: 9 AM to noon  
💰 Cost: ~$30 total (split between two, if you rent gear)

- **Why do it:** Paddling through the bay is a peaceful way to start your day. The water’s calm and clear — perfect for a leisurely morning.
- **Pro tip:** Rent the gear from Lighthouse Point and bring a cooler with snacks and drinks.

---

**Lunch: Bella's Pizza Co (Crestview)**
📍 *Where:* 106 E Main St  
💰 Cost: ~$25–$30 total

- **Why eat here:** It’s not just pizza — it’s a full-service Italian spot with great calzones, pastas, and wood-fired pizzas. The ambiance is cozy, and the prices are super reasonable.
- **Try this combo:** Half pizza + spaghetti with meatballs.

---

**Afternoon: Explore the Choctawhatchee River Trail**
📍 *Where:* Runs through downtown Crestview and into nearby towns  
⏰ Time: 1 PM to 3 PM  

- **Why do it:** This is a scenic trail that’s perfect for walking, biking, or just enjoying the local scenery. It connects with other trails if you want to keep going.
- **Pro tip:** Pack a light snack — there are no restaurants along this path.

---

**Dinner: Bistro 42 (Fort Walton Beach)**
📍 *Where:* 150 N Scenic Highway  
💰 Cost: ~$30–$40 total

- **Why eat here:** It’s a bit upscale, but still affordable if you keep it casual. They have a great selection of entrees like grilled salmon or pasta dishes.
- **Try this combo:** Grilled chicken with mashed potatoes + salad.

---

**Evening: Wrap Up at the Beach**
📍 *Where:* Back to Blue Wave or another nearby beach  
⏰ Time: 5 PM to sunset  

- **Why do it:** Spend your last evening soaking in the sun, watching the sky turn orange, and maybe even grabbing a bite from a food truck if you’re lucky enough to catch one.
