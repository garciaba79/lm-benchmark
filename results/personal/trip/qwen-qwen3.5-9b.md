### **🌴 Where to Stay: The "Sweet Spot" Motels**
Crestview itself is small, so your best bet for a relaxed stay with good amenities is actually just across the border in **Niceville** (about 10 mins away). Niceville has more options and slightly better parking near nature.

*   **Top Pick:** *Comfort Inn & Suites Crestview/Niceville* or similar budget chain motels along I-75/I-10.
*   **Why here?** You get a free breakfast (saves money!), comfortable bed for relaxation, and easy highway access to Wakulla Springs.
*   **Estimated Cost:** $65–$75/night.

### **🗓️ Day 1: The Local Foodie & Sunset Vibes**

**Morning (10:00 AM): Check-in & Hydrate**
Drop your bags at the motel. Go grab some water from a local store to save time later.

**Lunch (12:30 PM): The Creek Cafe** 🍳
You mentioned you love food, and this is the crown jewel of Crestview dining. It's cozy and famous for their savory breakfast dishes, but they serve lunch too.
*   **Order:** Go with a classic "Creek" sandwich or something like the Chicken Salad.
*   **Cost:** ~$18 (plus tip).

**Afternoon (3:00 PM): Chill & Wander**
Take a walk around the **Downtown Crestview Riverwalk**. It's small but perfect for people-watching and getting that "Florida small town" feel without spending a dime. Grab a coffee or iced tea at a local shop to sit and relax.

**Dinner (7:30 PM): Pizza Co. of Crestview** 🍕
If you want good food but don't want to break the bank, this spot is a solid choice. It's not "fine dining," but it's high-quality casual food that locals love.
*   **Order:** Try the "Meat Lover's" or just stick with a Margherita if you're watching calories.
*   **Cost:** ~$25–$30 (including drinks/tip).

---

### **🌲 Day 2: Nature & The Springs**

**Morning (8:30 AM): Wakulla Springs State Park** 🌊
This is the big attraction and it’s right down the road (about 15-20 mins drive from Niceville). It features one of the largest underground freshwater springs in North America. You can swim in cool, clear water and walk trails with incredible views.
*   **Cost:** ~$32–$38 (Day admission fee). *Tip: Book online a few days in advance to ensure you get in!*

**Lunch (12:30 PM): Pack a Picnic** 🥪
Instead of paying for lunch at the park, grab a sandwich or salad from your motel fridge (if allowed) or a nearby gas station (Wakulla Springs is close to some small stops). This saves about $15 compared to ordering in.

**Afternoon (3:00 PM): Relax on the Waterfront**
Drive down to **St. Marks State Park** or just head toward **Freeport Beach**. Even if you don't pay for a fancy beach club, finding a quiet public access point near the bay is perfect for relaxing, reading a book, and watching the sunset over the Gulf. It's very calming.
*   **Cost:** $0 (Public park areas).

**Late Afternoon (5:30 PM): The Farewell Treat**
Stop at a local ice cream parlor or a burger joint along I-10. Maybe grab some dessert for the road before heading home or back to your room.
*   **Cost:** ~$20.

### **🚗 A Few Realistic Tips from a Friend:**
1.  **Gas Note:** You said this excludes gas, but keep an eye on parking fees at Wakulla Springs if you drive a car (though usually included in admission). If you're driving your own vehicle, park there for free with your pass.
2.  **Water Safety:** The water is beautiful, but it's cold because it comes from underground! Wear a wetsuit or swimsuit that keeps you warm if you stay in the water long.
3.  **Flexibility:** If Wakulla Springs prices jump slightly, skip the lunch there and eat at a gas station for $10—that's smart money management so you have cash left for souvenirs.
4.  **Solo Travel Bonus:** If this is solo, I recommend getting a **room with a view** of the water or nature if it's in your budget. That alone can add relaxation value to your trip without extra cost on activities.
