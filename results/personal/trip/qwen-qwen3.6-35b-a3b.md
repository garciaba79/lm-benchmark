## 📅 Day 1: Adventure & Local Flavors

**Morning: Hike & Waterfall Hopping at West Florida Heritage Center & Nature Trails**
*   **Activity:** Start at the **West Florida Heritage Center** in Crestview. They have great walking trails through pine flatwoods and wetlands. It’s free! Pack a picnic.
*   **Why:** It’s peaceful, not too crowded, and gives you that "Florida wilderness" feel without the Everglades price tag.
*   **Cost:** $0

**Lunch: Casual & Delicious**
*   **Spot:** **The Old Towne Grille** or **Crestview Pizza Company**.
    *   *Friend Tip:* If you’re in the mood for something hearty, go to **The Old Towne Grille** for their daily specials. If you want quick, fun, and cheap, **Crestview Pizza Company** has amazing slices and calzones.
*   **Cost:** ~$15–$20 per person

**Afternoon: Kayaking on the Yellow River**
*   **Activity:** Rent a kayak or canoe from **Yellow River Canoe & Kayak** (just outside Crestview, about 10 mins south). Paddle through the scenic Yellow River. It’s calm, beautiful, and teeming with wildlife (birds, turtles, maybe even an alligator or two from a safe distance!).
*   **Cost:** ~$30–$40 for a 2-hour rental (split if you find a buddy, but solo is fine too).
*   **Alternative (Free):** If you have your own kayak, this is free! Just launch at the public access point nearby.

**Dinner: Southern Comfort**
*   **Spot:** **Crestview’s Best BBQ** or **The Smokehouse BBQ**.
    *   *Friend Tip:* You can’t visit NW Florida without good BBQ. Go to **The Smokehouse BBQ** for brisket that melts in your mouth. It’s casual, affordable, and delicious.
*   **Cost:** ~$20–$25 per person

**Evening: Stargazing & Chill**
*   **Activity:** Head to **Crestview City Park** or your accommodation’s porch. Grab a drink and just unwind. The sky is clear away from city lights.
*   **Cost:** $0 (or $10 for a couple of beers/wine)

## 📅 Day 2: Nature, Relaxation & Final Feasts

**Morning: Hiking at Point Washington State Forest**
*   **Activity:** Drive ~15 mins to **Point Washington State Forest**. Hike the **Longleaf Pine Trail** or the **Wildlife Trail**. It’s old-growth forest, incredibly peaceful, and has great birdwatching.
*   **Why:** It’s one of Florida’s most beautiful state forests. The trails are well-marked and not too strenuous.
*   **Cost:** $6 per vehicle (state park fee)

**Lunch: Picnic or Quick Bite**
*   **Activity:** Pack a picnic from Day 1’s leftovers or grab something from **Publix** (if you’re near one) or **Winn-Dixie**.
*   **Cost:** ~$10–$15

**Afternoon: Relax at Blue Spring or a Local Pool**
*   **Option A (Free/Nature):** Head to **Blue Spring State Park** (about 30 mins away) if you’re willing to drive a bit further. It’s stunning, but you’ll pay the $6 entry fee again.
*   **Option B (Budget-Friendly):** Visit **Crestview Community Center Pool** if it’s open (check hours). It’s cheap and a great way to cool off.
*   **Option C (Relaxing):** Find a quiet spot at **Crestview Lake Park**. Rent a paddleboat if you’re feeling fancy (~$20 for 30 mins), or just sit by the water.
*   **Cost:** $0–$20

**Late Afternoon: Coffee & Dessert**
*   **Spot:** **Crestview Coffee Roasters** or **The Cupcake Shop**.
    *   *Friend Tip:* Stop by **The Cupcake Shop** for a small dessert. It’s a local favorite and worth the splurge.
*   **Cost:** ~$10–$15

**Dinner: Final Feast**
*   **Spot:** **Hooters** (yes, really, but their wings are legit) or **Crestview’s Italian Restaurant** like **Bella Vita**.
    *   *Friend Tip:* If you want something special to end the trip, try **Bella Vita** for pasta. It’s cozy and romantic. If you want casual and fun, go back to **The Smokehouse BBQ** or try **Crestview’s Wings & Things**.
*   **Cost:** ~$25–$30 per person
