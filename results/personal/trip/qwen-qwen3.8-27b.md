### **Day 1: Beach Vibes & Local Eats**

**Morning: Hit the Beach Early**
*   **Where:** **Holly Beach** (It’s right there in Crestview, not the one in Destin!). It’s free to park and access.
*   **What to do:** Walk the shoreline, catch some waves, or just sit with a book and coffee. The sand is white, the water is blue, and it’s usually quiet before 9 AM.
*   **Cost:** $0 (plus your own coffee if you bring it from a gas station or local shop).

**Late Morning: Breakfast with a View**
*   **Where:** **The Biscayne** (or **Cafe du Monde** if you want something more casual).
*   **Why:** The Biscayne is right on the beach. You can order breakfast, sit outside, and watch the boats go by. It’s not super expensive, but it feels special.
*   **What to eat:** A classic Florida omelet or their famous shrimp & grits.
*   **Cost:** ~$15–$20 per person. Let’s say **$40** for two if you’re sharing or just grabbing a light meal.

**Afternoon: Chill & Explore**
*   **Where:** **Crestview City Park** or **Fanning Springs State Park** (if you’re up for a short drive).
*   **Option A (Easy Mode):** Rent a kayak or paddleboard in Holly Beach. It’s cheap and fun.
    *   **Cost:** ~$25–$30/hr. Let’s do 1 hour for two people: **$30**.
*   **Option B (Nature Mode):** Drive to Fanning Springs (~20 mins). It’s a natural spring with clear water. Great for a swim or just hanging out in the woods.
    *   **Cost:** Entry is like $5–$6 per person. **$10 total**.
*   *Let’s go with the kayak for the "active" vibe.*

**Evening: Dinner & Sunset**
*   **Where:** **The Wharf Restaurant** (in Pensacola, but only 15 mins away – worth it for the food) OR stick local at **Papa John’s Pizza & Grill** (don’t judge, it’s a local favorite in Crestview and super affordable).
*   **Let’s go local to save money:** **Papa John’s** or **The Griddle House** (great for casual, tasty food without the fine-dining price tag).
*   **What to eat:** Burgers, fries, maybe a local beer or wine.
*   **Cost:** ~$25–$30 per person. **$60 total** for two.

**Nightcap:**
*   Walk back to the beach or sit on your balcony. No cost.

**Day 1 Total Estimate:**

### **Day 2: Adventure & Hidden Gems**

**Morning: Hike & Sip**
*   **Where:** **Taylorsville Creek** or **Deer Lake Park** (Crestview).
*   **What to do:** Go on a short hike. Deer Lake Park is huge, has trails, and is very peaceful. Bring a thermos of coffee or tea.
*   **Cost:** $0 (parking is usually free or minimal).

**Late Morning: Brunch**
*   **Where:** **Mama’s Kitchen** (Crestview).
*   **Why:** It’s a local institution. The food is hearty, affordable, and the vibe is super friendly. Try the biscuits and gravy or the fried chicken plate.
*   **Cost:** ~$12–$15 per person. **$30 total**.

**Afternoon: Unique Experience**
*   **Option A (Fun):** **Crestview Tiki Bar & Grill** for a mid-afternoon cocktail. It’s a relaxed, outdoor spot with a great atmosphere.
*   **Option B (Culture):** Visit the **Okaloosa Museum of Art** (if you’re feeling cultured) or just browse the **Crestview Farmers Market** (if it’s Saturday/Sunday).
*   **Let’s go with the Farmers Market** (it’s usually on weekends, check times). It’s a great place to find local treats, souvenirs, and cheap eats.
    *   **Cost:** $10–$15 for a nice souvenir or snack. **$15 total**.

**Late Afternoon: Snacks & Sweets**
*   **Where:** **The Ice Cream Factory** or **Baskin Robbins** (yes, really – it’s a Florida staple).
*   **What to do:** Get two scoops of ice cream while walking around town.
*   **Cost:** **$10 total**.

**Evening: Farewell Dinner**
*   **Where:** **Seaport Steakhouse** (Pensacola, 15 mins away) OR **The Old Port** (Crestview, if open).
*   **Let’s go with Seaport** for a nicer end. It’s a bit splurgey but worth it for the view and the steak.
*   **What to eat:** A ribeye or salmon, plus a glass of wine.
*   **Cost:** ~$40–$50 per person. **$90 total**.

**Nightcap:**
*   One last stroll on the beach or a quiet drink at the bar.
*   **Cost:** $10 for a beer or wine. **$10 total**.

**Day 2 Total Estimate:**
*   Brunch: $30
*   Farmers Market/Souvenir: $15

4.  **Sunset is Free:** Don’t overschedule. The best moments in Crestview are the quiet ones at dusk on the beach.
