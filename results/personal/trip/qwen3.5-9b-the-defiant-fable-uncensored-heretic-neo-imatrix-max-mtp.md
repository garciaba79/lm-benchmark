### **Day 1: Saturday – Nature & Local Flavors**
**Morning:**  
Start early at **Crestview Beach Park**. It’s free, has a nice beach area (though the water is more for swimming in summer), walking paths, and picnic spots. Grab a coffee to go from **Starbucks on 2nd Ave NE** ($5) or skip it if you’re saving up.  
- *Cost:* $0–$5

**Lunch:**  
Head over to **The Rusty Spoon** (1406 N 3rd St). It’s a local favorite with Southern comfort food, great coffee, and a relaxed vibe. Try their fried chicken sandwich or shrimp & grits.  
- *Cost:* ~$20–$25

**Afternoon:**  
Rent bikes from **Crestview Bike Shop** (if available) or just walk around the historic downtown area. Check out:
- The **Crestview Public Library** (free, charming old building with a nice courtyard).
- A quick stroll through **Downtown Crestview**, where you’ll see local art and small shops.

If you want something more active, drive 15 minutes west to **Wakulla Springs State Park**. You can take the free guided boat tour (check seasonal hours) or just walk along the boardwalk near the springs.  
- *Cost:* Free for walking; boat tours ~$20/person if you do it

**Dinner:**  
End your day at **Burger & Co.** (1305 N 3rd St). It’s a local chain with solid burgers, fries, and shakes—perfect after a long day. Order the classic burger and a milkshake to keep costs down.  
- *Cost:* ~$25–$30

**Evening:**  
Grab a cheap beer or soda at **The Rusty Spoon** patio (if open) or just walk around downtown under the stars. No need for fancy entertainment here—Crestview’s charm is in its simplicity.  
- *Cost:* $5–$10

### **Day 2: Sunday – Chill Vibes & Fresh Air**
**Morning:**  
Sleep in a bit, then head to **Wakulla Springs State Park** again (if you didn’t go yesterday). This time, rent a kayak or canoe from the park’s rental shop. The water is crystal clear and surrounded by lush forest—it’s peaceful and great for photos.  
- *Cost:* Kayak rental ~$30–$40

**Lunch:**  
Drive 15 minutes south to **Gulf Breeze** (still close enough, and it has better options). Grab a quick bite at **The Fish House** or **Burger & Co.** again if you’re feeling nostalgic. Or try **Taco Time**, a local spot with affordable tacos and chips.  
- *Cost:* ~$20–$25

**Afternoon:**  
Head back to Crestview for some relaxation:
- Visit the **Crestview Historical Museum** (free, small but interesting).
- Walk through **Henderson Park**, a local green space with playgrounds and walking trails.
- If you’re feeling adventurous, drive 20 minutes north to **St. Marks National Wildlife Refuge**. You can take a free guided tour or just walk the boardwalks along the marshes (bring binoculars if you have them).  
- *Cost:* Free

**Dinner:**  
Wrap up your trip with a cozy meal at **The Rusty Spoon** again—they’re great for repeat visits. Try their mac & cheese or a salad to keep it light but satisfying.  
- *Cost:* ~$20–$25
