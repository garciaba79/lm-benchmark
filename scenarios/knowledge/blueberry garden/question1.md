I've been getting into blueberry gardening this year. Planted 6 bushes (three varieties: Emerald, Jewel, and Star) in raised beds with acidic soil mix (pH 4.5–5.5). Used pine bark mulch to keep moisture in and weeds down. So far, the plants are doing well—new growth is strong, no major pests yet (just a few aphids, sprayed with neem oil). First small harvest expected next spring. Cost so far: $120 for plants, $45 for soil/mulch from Lowe's, $30 for drip irrigation setup. Goal is to have enough berries for fresh eating, jam, and freezing by year 3. Need to remember to fertilize with ammonium sulfate next month and protect from birds with netting.
